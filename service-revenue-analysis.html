Import Weekly Skimmer Revenue Reports

ADMIN and SYSTEM_ADMIN upload both Skimmer Invoice Detail CSV reports for the same custom date range. Invoices.csv supplies the financial totals; Line Items.csv supplies the revenue classification.
Drop Invoices.csv Here
Skimmer → Reports → Invoice Reports → Invoice Details → Invoices → Custom Date Range → Export → CSV
No Invoices.csv selected.
Drop Line Items.csv Here
Skimmer → Reports → Invoice Reports → Invoice Details → Line Items → Custom Date Range → Export → CSV
No Line Items.csv selected.
The two files must cover the same custom date range and the same invoice set. The system imports Invoices.csv first, then joins Line Items.csv by Invoice # to classify Pool Service, Service, PGP, Projects, and review items.
