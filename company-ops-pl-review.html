Import Rules & Safeguards

Protecting the original accounting record

The importer reads selectable text from landscape FYTD Income Statement pages, extracting Service and Projects monthly actual/budget totals plus detailed accounts. It checks the arithmetic before allowing the data to be saved.

Scanned PDFs and unfamiliar report layouts stop for review rather than silently storing inaccurate figures.

New uploads create dated, auditable versions. The most recently completed import becomes the current view for overlapping months; prior imports are retained.

The PDF is stored in a private, admin-only storage bucket.
