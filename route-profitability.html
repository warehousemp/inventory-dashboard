Route Profitability

Weekly Route cost-to-service analysis combining recurring route revenue, PPV labor, Skimmer chemical usage and Samsara mileage. This page is restricted to Admin and System Admin.

Reporting Assumptions

Fuel assumptions are adjustable
Revenue is converted from monthly route revenue to a weekly equivalent using monthly × 12 ÷ 52. PPV labor comes from the PPV pay-period visit totals and approved PPV rates. Chemical costs come from the selected Skimmer Service History week. Fuel is estimated as miles ÷ MPG × fuel price. Until take-home/commute mileage rules are configured by vehicle, Samsara miles shown here should be treated as an operating estimate rather than final accounting fuel expense.
