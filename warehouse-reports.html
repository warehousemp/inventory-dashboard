The 2026 Annual Filter & O-Ring Changeout is scheduled Monday through Friday at up to 4 client locations per Route Tech per changeout day, beginning October 5 and completing by October 31. Weekend service completions are not scheduled as filter-changeout days. Use Changeout Service Date to see exactly what Warehouse must stage for that date. CSC Billing List

CSC annual filter-changeout billing checklist. Total Billable to Client is calculated from the Hurricane filter and O-ring costs in the current parts database using (Hurricane Part Unit Cost × Filter Qty + O-Ring Unit Cost × O-Ring Qty) × 1.82, then rounded to the nearest whole dollar. Both unit costs are shown for billing verification. Rows showing TBD, COST NEEDED, or REVIEW require review before entry into Skimmer.
