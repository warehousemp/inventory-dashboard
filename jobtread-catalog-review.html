Before enabling the nightly sync

  1. Audit all existing JobTread item IDs and reconcile exact part-number matches.
  2. Resolve additional order-on-demand parts and stale deadstock flags.
  3. Validate JobTread cost code 1410 and tax-inclusive Unit Cost. Selling-price formulas are out of scope.
  4. Review a change-only dry run, then explicitly enable synchronization.

All JobTread catalog mappings are disabled for writing by default.
