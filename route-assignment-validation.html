How to Get the Route Assignments PDF

Skimmer workflow
  1. In Skimmer, open Routes.
  2. Select Route Assignments.
  3. Open More Options.
  4. Change the date to next week Friday.
  5. Click the Print button in the upper-right corner above the map.
  6. Choose Print to PDF and save the PDF on your Desktop. Include the Friday date in the filename when possible, such as 2026_0828 Route Assignments.pdf.
  7. Drag and drop the saved PDF into the box on this page.
