Skimmer Work Order Creation Queue

Production work orders are processed one scheduled changeout date at a time. Each queue item equals one client location and one changeout date. Multiple filter systems at the same property are combined into one Skimmer work order. SERVICE and WAREHOUSE users can view this queue. Queue management and date processing are limited to Service Manager, Admin, and System Admin.

Process Work Orders by Changeout Date

CSC processes one date at a time. Only READY records for that date are sent to Skimmer. The batch pauses immediately if a record needs review. CREATED, HOLD, ERROR, and SKIPPED records are never resubmitted.
