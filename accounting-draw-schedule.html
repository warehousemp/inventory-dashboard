How to read these numbers: Scheduled $ comes from customer invoices attached to JobTread draw tasks, including drafts. A blank amount means JobTread has not supplied a linked invoice amount; it does not mean $0. Invoiced $ excludes drafts; Outstanding $ and Collected $ use the values JobTread reports on issued invoices. All amounts are grouped by the draw's current scheduled date, not the invoice or payment transaction date. Changes are detected during nightly sync, so moves that happen and revert between syncs may not be visible in history. This page never edits JobTread.
